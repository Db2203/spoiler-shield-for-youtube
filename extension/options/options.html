Welcome

Spoiler Shield is installed and already protecting you with the GTA 6 keyword list below. Three things worth knowing:

  • Strong keywords hide a video, Short, comment, post or search suggestion on their own. Weak keywords only count when two different ones appear together, so a common first name alone never hides anything.
  • If you open a matching video, Short or channel, playback stops and the page is covered by a Go back screen.
  • Thumbnails can spoil without a single word. Turn on Blur all thumbnails below if you want them hidden until you click.

The toolbar icon shows how many items were hidden on the current page. Click it for quick switches; come back here to edit your lists.
